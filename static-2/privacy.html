Privacy Policy

Public pilot · last updated November 2026

KORA is a student pilot and collects the minimum needed to run the marketplace. We do not sell your data. This policy explains what we store and why, and is written to align with the principles of India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Account details — your name, phone number, role and, optionally, a GST number.
  • Marketplace activity — produce listings, bids and service requests you create.
  • One-time codes — used only to verify your phone at sign-in, and expired after use.

How we use it

Your data is used only to operate the pilot: to sign you in, show listings and bids to the relevant parties, and connect requests with suppliers. We do not use it for advertising and we do not share it with third parties for marketing.

Consent

We ask for your explicit consent to these terms when you register. You can withdraw consent by deleting your account, which removes your personal data and related records.

Storage and security

In this pilot, data is stored in a local database on the operator's machine. Access is role-based, and one-time codes are single-use with a short expiry. No payment or banking data is collected during the pilot.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data. Account deletion is available in-app and removes your listings, bids and requests.
