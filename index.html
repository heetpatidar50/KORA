WHY NOW

Agriculture is changing

Fruit and vegetable growers keep only a fraction of what shoppers pay, while perishables rot between farm and market. Demographics, connectivity and AI are converging — India needs an intelligence layer for agriculture, not just another marketplace.

KORA AI

The intelligence engine of the platform — not a chatbot bolt-on. A copilot for Indian agriculture that presents forecasts as ranges with confidence, not single numbers you can't trust.

MODULE A

Demand & price forecasting

Uncertainty-aware estimates of what buyers will need, where and when — low / central / high scenarios with a visible accuracy history.

MODULE B

Direct buyer marketplace

Bid-based matching on crop, quantity, grade, distance and delivery window. Every accepted order recorded as a documented transaction.

MODULE C

Pooled, spoilage-aware logistics

Compatible orders combined and routed to cut empty trips and spoilage — planning on deadlines and shelf life, not distance alone.

MODULE D

Transparent price & payment

Every deduction itemised, from farm-gate price to the buyer's final price, with payment status, quality records and dispute workflow.
